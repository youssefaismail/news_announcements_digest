# Severe Weather Warning: Classes Suspended This Afternoon

The National Weather Service has issued a severe thunderstorm warning for the campus area effective 2 PM to 6 PM. All afternoon classes and outdoor activities are suspended. Students and staff should seek shelter in the nearest campus building and avoid unnecessary travel.
