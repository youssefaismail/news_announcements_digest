# Gas Leak Detected Near Engineering Annex — Building Closed

A gas leak was detected near the Engineering Annex this morning. The building is closed until further notice while utility crews investigate and repair the issue. Classes scheduled in the Annex are relocated to the Science Building, Room 110, for the remainder of the day.
