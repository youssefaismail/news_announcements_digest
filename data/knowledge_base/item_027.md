# Fall 2026 Course Registration Closes Friday

Registration for Fall 2026 courses closes this Friday at 11:59 PM. Students who have not registered or confirmed their schedule will be assessed a $75 late registration fee and may lose priority seating in required courses. Advisors are available for walk-in appointments in the Student Services building through Thursday.
