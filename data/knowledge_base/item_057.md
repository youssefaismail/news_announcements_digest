# Mandatory Freshman Advising Sessions This Week

All first-year students must attend one advising session this week to finalize their course schedules. Sessions run Monday through Thursday, 9 AM to 4 PM, in the Advising Center, Room 210. Bring your student ID and a printed copy of your proposed schedule.
